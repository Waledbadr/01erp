# VAT and ZATCA rules

Use a transaction-time tax snapshot and exact decimal calculations. Round each line's VAT and total half-up to two decimals. Support inclusive and exclusive pricing, credit/debit adjustments, and B2B/B2C document classification. ZATCA submission is asynchronous with durable status and retry; never claim compliance before external onboarding and certification. See skills/saudi-vat/SKILL.md.
